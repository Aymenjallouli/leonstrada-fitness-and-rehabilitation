What Dave coaches

Built for one person at a time.

Every programme starts from your day-to-day life and your goals, whether that is a first session in years, a wedding date, or getting back to the gym after surgery.

01

Strength

Tailored workout plans and goals set at a pace you can keep. Sessions are challenging but enjoyable, and built around you.

  • 1-to-1
  • Tailored plans
02

Mobility

Strength and mobility training with one aim: a better quality of life. Moving freely, lifting comfortably, trusting your body again.

  • 1-to-1
  • Outdoor sessions
03

Rehabilitation

Returning after illness, an operation or a long break. Sessions start where your body is today and build from there, at the pace it allows.

Your coach

Black belt. Patient coach.

Dave is a personal trainer and black-belt kickboxing instructor. Before Leonstrada he ran Kickfit Coaching for almost ten years, teaching kickboxing and self-defence alongside personal training.

Today the focus is quality of life: strength and mobility work, nutritional advice and programmes shaped around how you actually live. Clients describe him as encouraging, observant and always ready to listen.
